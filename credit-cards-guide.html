Understanding 0% Balance Transfer Credit Cards in Australia

Updated: August 2026

If you're carrying existing credit card debt, a 0% balance transfer offer can be an effective way to stop paying high interest charges and clear your principal balance faster.

How Does a Balance Transfer Work?

A balance transfer allows you to move existing high-interest credit card debt over to a new credit card that offers an introductory 0% p.a. interest rate for a fixed promotional period (e.g., 12 to 32 months).

Key Tip: During the promotional period, every dollar you pay goes directly toward paying off the balance rather than paying interest charges.

Important Things to Consider Before Applying

  • Balance Transfer Fees: Many issuers charge an upfront fee (typically between 1% and 3% of the amount transferred).
  • Reversion Rate: Once the 0% promotional period ends, any remaining balance will be charged at a much higher purchase or cash-advance rate.
  • New Purchases: Avoid using the new card for new everyday purchases, as these will often incur regular high interest rates.

Is a Balance Transfer Right for You?

If you have a clear plan to pay off the transferred amount before the introductory period ends, a balance transfer card can save you hundreds—or even thousands—of dollars in interest.
