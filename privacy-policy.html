Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

At AusFinanceHub (accessible from ausfinancehub.github.io), accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by AusFinanceHub and how we use it, in compliance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our financial comparison engine or submit a request for guidance via our consultation form, we may collect personal information including but not limited to:

  • Full Legal Name
  • Email Address and Phone Number
  • Financial product preferences (e.g., Credit Cards, Savings Accounts, Personal Loans)
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and site usage patterns collected automatically via cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various professional purpose, including:

  • To evaluate and route your consultation requests to relevant partners or services.
  • To operate, maintain, and enhance our website speed, interface, and features.
  • To analyze user behavior and optimize our financial comparison engine.
  • To send updates or answer inquiries directly submitted via email or contact forms.
  • To comply with regulatory obligations and legal requirements.

3. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Disclosure

AusFinanceHub acts as an independent financial product comparison engine. Our site contains outbound affiliate links to financial institutions, credit card providers, and lenders. If you click on an outbound link and complete an application on a partner site, we may receive a commission or referral fee. Please note that we do not control third-party websites, and they operate under their own independent privacy policies.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

AusFinanceHub uses standard 'cookies' to store information about visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This information is used to optimize user experience by customizing web page content based on browser type or other technical metrics.

5. Data Protection and Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee complete security of data transmitted online.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under Australian privacy laws, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

7. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by email at Ausfinancehub.au@gmail.com.
