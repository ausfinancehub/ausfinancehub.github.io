Privacy Policy

At AusFinanceHub, accessible from https://ausfinancehub.github.io, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by AusFinanceHub and how we use it.

If you contact us directly or fill out our contact/consultation form, we may receive personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and details regarding your financial product interest.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, provide personalized recommendations for financial products, and improve our services.

Third-Party Financial Services

AusFinanceHub provides links to third-party banking and financial services (e.g., Qantas Money, CommBank, Westpac, ANZ). We do not control their privacy policies and encourage you to review their terms before applying.
