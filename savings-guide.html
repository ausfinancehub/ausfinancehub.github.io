Top High-Interest Savings Accounts in Australia

Updated: August 2026

Finding the right savings account in Australia can significantly boost your annual interest earnings. With interest rates fluctuating across major banks, choosing an account with low fees and high bonus rates is critical.

1. Westpac Life Savings Account

Westpac offers competitive bonus rates for account holders who meet monthly deposit conditions and make regular growing deposits. It's an ideal choice for young adults and everyday savers looking for flexibility.

2. Macquarie Savings Account

Macquarie Bank provides strong variable interest rates without strict monthly deposit criteria, making it a popular choice for hassle-free savings.

Key Factors to Consider Before Opening an Account

  • Monthly Deposit Rules: Ensure you can meet minimum deposit limits each month to qualify for bonus interest rates.
  • Withdrawal Penalties: Some banks forfeit your bonus interest for the month if you make a withdrawal.
  • Government Guarantee: Australian ADIs (Authorised Deposit-taking Institutions) protect deposits up to $250,000 per individual.
